What is Gativra?

Gativra is AI software that runs the physical side of logistics. It turns the cameras, sensors and weighbridges already on a site into one system that reads every truck, container and pallet, decides what should happen and acts on it in under a second.

The name joins two Sanskrit words: Gati, speed, and Vara, a breeze: logistics that moves fast and flows smoothly.
